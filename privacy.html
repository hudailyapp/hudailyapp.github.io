Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Hudaily is a habit tracker for Muslims, with prayer times, Qibla, du’a and adhkar. This policy explains what the app stores and where information goes. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your habits and notes stay on your phone.

The short version

  • There is no account or sign-in. We never ask for your email, phone number or password.
  • Your name, habits, check-ins, notes and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • We do not show ads, sell information, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your approximate location is used to work out prayer times, the Qibla direction and nearby mosques.

What is stored on your phone

Hudaily saves the following on your device so the app works: the name you type, the goal you choose, your habits and daily check-ins, notes you write on a habit, your dhikr counts and history, your reminder times and your settings.

We cannot see this information, because Hudaily never sends it anywhere. If you back up your phone (for example with iCloud or Google backup), the backup may include it, under your phone provider’s own rules.

Location

Hudaily asks to use your location only while the app is open. It is used for three things: prayer times, the Qibla direction and finding nearby mosques. The app asks for approximate location, not your exact position.

Prayer times and the Qibla direction are worked out on your phone. We do not store your location, and we do not run a server to send it to.

If you say no, Hudaily uses London as a default so the app still works. You can change your choice at any time in your phone’s settings.

Services the app connects to

A few features use outside services. They receive only what is needed to answer the request and, like any website, they can also see your IP address.

  • Nearby mosques: your approximate coordinates and a search distance are sent to the Overpass API (overpass-api.de), a public service that searches OpenStreetMap data. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Names of Allah: the list is downloaded from the Aladhan API (api.aladhan.com) and kept on your phone. No personal information is sent.
  • Directions: if you tap Get Directions or search for mosques in Maps, Hudaily opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with a location in the link. What happens next is covered by their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Prayer-time alerts and habit reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. We do not use a notification server and we do not collect any device token. You can turn them off in Settings, or in your phone’s notification settings.

Purchases

Hudaily Premium is a subscription sold through the Apple App Store (or Google Play). Apple or Google handles your payment, and we never see your card details.

To check that your Premium is active, we use RevenueCat, a subscription service. It receives purchase information such as your receipt and an anonymous identifier, not your name or your habits. See RevenueCat’s own privacy policy for details.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

Hudaily is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Hudaily does not collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Because everything is on your phone, you are in control. “Reset Streak & Progress” in Settings erases your habits, notes, history and settings from Hudaily, and deleting the app removes the rest. Nothing is kept on our side.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and we will tell you in the app if the change is important.
