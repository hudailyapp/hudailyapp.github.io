Common questions

How does my streak work?

A day counts when you complete at least one habit. Your streak is the number of days in a row that count. If you miss a day it starts again from zero, but your best streak is always kept.

How are prayer times worked out?

Hudaily calculates them on your phone from your location, using the method and Asr setting in Settings → Prayer Calculation Method. The default is Muslim World League with the standard Asr. Change it to match your local mosque or authority.

My prayer times or Qibla look wrong

Allow location access for Hudaily in your phone’s settings. When location is off, Hudaily uses London as a default, so the times shown are London’s. Also check that your calculation method matches your area.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Turn on Enable Notifications (or Evening Reminder) and allow notifications for Hudaily in your phone’s settings. Battery-saver modes on some Android phones can delay or block them.

How do prayer time alerts work?

Turn on Prayer Time Alerts in Settings, pick which prayers you want, and choose whether to be notified at the prayer time or a few minutes before. Hudaily schedules the next ten days or so on your phone, using the same location and calculation method as your prayer times, and tops them up every time you open the app. So open Hudaily at least once a week or two to keep them coming. If you travel, open the app once in the new place so the times update.

How do I add or remove a habit?

Open the Habits tab and tap + to add one from the suggestions. Open any habit and tap the bin to delete it. The free plan tracks up to 5 habits; Premium has no limit.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. Hudaily has no accounts and does not upload your habits or history anywhere. If you delete the app or use Reset, it is gone. One exception: when you open the Mosque tool, your approximate location is sent to OpenStreetMap’s public search service to find mosques near you.
