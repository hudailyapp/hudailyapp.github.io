About Hudaily

Hudaily is a habit tracker for Muslims. It includes prayer times, a Qibla compass, du’a and adhkar, a tasbeeh counter, a Hijri calendar, prayer alerts and a nearby mosque finder.

Religious content and accuracy

Hudaily is a reminder and a helper, not a source of religious rulings. Please keep in mind:

  • Prayer times are calculated from your location and the method and madhab you choose. They can differ from your local mosque. If in doubt, follow your local mosque or scholars.
  • The Qibla compass depends on your phone’s sensors and can be thrown off by magnets, cases and metal. Check it against a trusted source if you can.
  • Du’a, adhkar and Arabic texts are included as reminders. Please check them against trusted sources, and do not treat the app as a fatwa.
  • Notifications can be delayed or silenced by your phone’s settings, battery saver or Focus modes. Please do not rely on them alone.
